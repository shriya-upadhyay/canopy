# Canopy — Nano Banana prompt pack

**Deck:** Raingentic Commerce Hackathon NYC · 4-minute pitch
**Product:** Canopy — conditional settlement for agent commerce

---

## The metaphor map

Use this so every image means something. Nothing decorative.

| Visual | Concept |
|---|---|
| The canopy layer | The marketplace — where agents exchange |
| Rain falling through leaves | Payments flowing (and the sponsor) |
| Rain caught, not reaching the floor | Settlement withheld — the $0 case |
| Shafts of light, partial | Partial settlement |
| Roots / anchored trunk | The seller's bond, staked |
| Mycorrhizal network, glowing threads underground | Agent-to-agent settlement rails |
| New growth rings, a branch extending | Earned credit limit expanding |
| Canopy edge meeting open sky | The Rain boundary — leaving the marketplace |

---

## House style — paste this into EVERY prompt

```
Style: cinematic macro photography of a rainforest canopy, deep emerald and
moss green, wet dark bark, volumetric mist, shafts of warm golden light
filtering through layered leaves. Rich shadow, high dynamic range, shallow
depth of field. Subtle bioluminescent teal accents suggesting data flow.
Dark, premium, editorial — like a Kew Gardens documentary shot by a fintech
brand. NOT clip art, NOT stock-photo bright, NOT cartoon.
16:9 aspect ratio. Leave the left third as clean negative space for text.
```

**Two rules for Nano Banana:** ask for images with **negative space** and add
your own text in Keynote/Figma. It renders 3–6 words reliably, not paragraphs.
Generate each slide 3–4 times and keep the best; it varies a lot.

## Slide 3 — The mechanism

```
A single enormous leaf holding a pool of rainwater, tilted so a measured
portion spills over one edge while the rest is retained. Extreme macro,
water tension visible, gold rim light on the spilling stream. A sense of
deliberate, precise measurement.
[HOUSE STYLE]
```

Overlay: **Sign a maximum. Settle on the outcome.** `$0.50 → $0.20`

## Slide 4 — The money shot ($0, no transaction)

```
A broad waxy rainforest leaf holding rainwater completely still — not one drop
falling, the forest floor beneath it bone dry and lit by a thin shaft of light.
Absolute stillness. The absence of movement is the subject.
[HOUSE STYLE]
```

Overlay: **Wrong strategy → $0. No on-chain transaction.**
*Nobody had to arbitrate that.*

> This is your strongest slide. Sit on it for a beat.

## One thing to avoid

Don't put a raincloud on every slide or lean on the Rain pun verbally. The
imagery should make the connection; saying it out loud reads as pandering to
the sponsor. Let the judges notice it themselves.
